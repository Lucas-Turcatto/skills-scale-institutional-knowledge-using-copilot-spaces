# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads own the solution architecture and technical strategy. They collaborate with Product and Project Managers to ensure scalability, maintainability, and alignment with organizational standards.

### Responsibilities
- Design solution architecture and technical approaches
- Establish coding standards, testing practices, and architectural principles
- Conduct and facilitate technical reviews
- Identify and escalate technical risks and dependencies
- Mentor team members on technical best practices
- Coordinate with Operations/DevOps on deployment and infrastructure requirements

### Goals
- Deliver scalable, maintainable, and secure solutions
- Build team capability and technical maturity
- Reduce technical debt and maintenance burden

### Typical Communication
- Design review meetings and architecture documentation
- Weekly sync with Project Manager and Product Manager
- Technical risk updates in risk register

### How They Interact with Existing Roles
- **With Developers**: Provides architectural guidance, code review leadership, and mentorship on technical best practices
- **With Product Managers**: Aligns technical approach with product vision and communicates trade-offs
- **With Project Managers**: Escalates technical risks and dependencies that impact timelines

---

## QA / Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA and Testing Leads define quality standards, test strategy, and acceptance criteria validation. They ensure products meet acceptance criteria and quality gates before release.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy and quality standards for projects
- Collaborate with developers on acceptance criteria and testability
- Design and execute manual and automated testing
- Manage testing tools, environments, and CI/CD quality gates
- Validate acceptance criteria before marking work as done
- Identify and escalate quality risks

### Goals
- Prevent defects from reaching production
- Enable fast, confident releases
- Reduce bug-escape rates and post-release incidents

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and acceptance criteria refinement
- QA reports and quality metrics in weekly syncs
- Release readiness sign-offs

### How They Interact with Existing Roles
- **With Developers**: Partners on test automation, testability design, and acceptance criteria clarity
- **With Product Managers**: Validates feature acceptance and advises on quality trade-offs
- **With Project Managers**: Provides quality metrics and release readiness assessments

---

## Scrum Master / Delivery Facilitator

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters or Delivery Facilitators enable team execution by managing ceremonies, removing blockers, and maintaining team health. They focus on process optimization and team velocity.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate daily standups, planning, and retrospective ceremonies
- Track team capacity and sprint commitments
- Help identify and remove blockers escalating as needed
- Maintain project board and workflow visibility
- Coach the team on agile practices and continuous improvement
- Support Project Manager with execution metrics and burndown tracking

### Goals
- Maximize team velocity and predictability
- Create psychological safety and high-performing teams
- Reduce cycle time and unplanned work

### Typical Communication
- Daily facilitation of ceremonies
- Blocker escalation to Project Manager
- Velocity reports and retrospective action items

### How They Interact with Existing Roles
- **With Developers**: Removes impediments, facilitates ceremonies, and coaches agile practices
- **With Product Managers**: Coordinates backlog refinement and sprint planning
- **With Project Managers**: Provides execution data and supports team capacity management

---

## Executive Sponsor / Stakeholder

### Role Summary
Executive Sponsors represent organizational leadership and provide strategic direction, resource decisions, and escalation authority for projects.

### Responsibilities
- Approve project charter and initiation
- Make go/no-go decisions at key gates
- Remove organizational blockers and secure resources
- Provide strategic context and business objectives
- Receive and act on escalations outside team authority
- Approve major scope or timeline changes

### Goals
- Ensure project alignment with organizational strategy
- Minimize external blockers and delays
- Enable rapid decision-making at governance gates

### Typical Communication
- Project initiation and gate reviews
- Monthly stakeholder updates
- Ad-hoc escalations requiring executive decision

### How They Interact with Existing Roles
- **With Project Managers**: Receives escalations, approves major decisions, and removes organizational blockers
- **With Product Managers**: Provides strategic context and approves prioritization decisions
- **Organizational Interface**: Secures cross-functional resources and resolves conflicts outside project scope

---

## Operations / DevOps Lead

### Role Summary
Operations and DevOps Leaders manage infrastructure, deployment pipelines, monitoring, and production stability. They ensure reliable delivery and observability.

### Responsibilities
- Design and maintain CI/CD pipelines and deployment automation
- Manage infrastructure, environments, and scalability
- Implement monitoring, alerting, and observability solutions
- Support pre-release testing and smoke test execution
- Manage rollback and incident response procedures
- Collaborate with Technical Leads on infrastructure requirements

### Goals
- Enable fast, safe, automated deployments
- Maintain production stability and uptime
- Reduce deployment cycle time and manual effort

### Typical Communication
- Pre-release checklists and deployment coordination
- Infrastructure requirement discussions in planning
- Production incidents and rollback decisions
- Post-deployment verification and metrics

### How They Interact with Existing Roles
- **With Developers**: Provides infrastructure support, deployment guidance, and observability tools
- **With Technical Leads**: Collaborates on infrastructure design and operational requirements
- **With Project Managers**: Coordinates deployment schedules and provides production stability metrics
